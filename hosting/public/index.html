Status Saver

View and save image and video statuses already stored on your device.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Browse image and video statuses saved on your device
  • Save the ones you want to your gallery
  • Share or repost saved media
  • Start a chat without saving the number to your contacts

Privacy

The app works locally on your phone. We do not run servers that collect or store your personal data, and your saved media stays on your device.
